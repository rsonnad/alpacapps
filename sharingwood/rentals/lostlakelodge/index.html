The Lost Lake Lodge

$4,500 /mo

22307 E Lost Lake Rd, Snohomish, WA 98296

4 bd · 2.5 ba · 2,340 sqft · Available Dec 1

About this home

Beautifully maintained, multi-level home nestled among the trees near Lost Lake, between Woodinville and Monroe — spacious living areas, natural light, and a unique blend of privacy, nature, and community.

The main floor features an open-concept kitchen with granite countertops and an island, a great room with a distinctive river-rock wood-burning fireplace, and large windows and skylights. French doors open to a covered patio and paver-stone outdoor living area. A bonus room, single-car garage, spacious laundry room with storage, and a 3/4 bathroom complete this level. Solid oak flooring runs throughout, with carpeted stairs.

Upstairs are three bedrooms and a full bathroom with a soaking tub, dual sinks, and a newly remodeled shower. A large grassy community area is just outside. The top floor offers a versatile room with skylights and shades, a large window with a window seat, built-in storage, and a compact full bathroom — ideal for a bedroom, office, or crafting space.

Sharingwood is a cohousing neighborhood of 29 homes across 23 acres of greenbelt, with a common house, play structures, a greenhouse, and a community garden. Residents participate in teams and volunteer a few hours a month, enjoying community connection while keeping privacy in their own homes.
