The home

Light, warmth and room to breathe

This lower unit of a modern single-family home was built with real care for craft. Big windows pour light across warm hardwood floors and an open living space that flows from the kitchen to a reading corner to the dining table.

It comes fully furnished, down to the dishware, linens and a high-quality sound system wired to Alexa. There's a large bedroom, a smaller room for an office, guest or studio, generous closets and cupboards, and a separate large storage room.

Step out to the patio for long summer evenings. In winter, the three-person cedar sauna is a few steps away.

The neighborhood

Live inside Sharingwood

Sharingwood is one of the oldest co-housing communities in Washington: a cluster of homes in the woods, built around shared space and neighbors who actually know each other. It isn't religious or ideological. It's simply about taking part.

  • A common house for shared meals, cooking and gatherings
  • A large play field and community gardens
  • A forested greenbelt with trails and small creeks to cross
  • Great for growing families, or anyone who wants real community
