5. Continuing Work-Off Terms

  • Contractor will continue to log all work performed at the Alpaca Playhouse via the alpacapps time-tracking system, with clock-in/clock-out timestamps and a task reference for each entry.
  • The labor rate for credit-against-balance purposes is fixed at $40.00 per hour.
  • Time entries are subject to Owner review and approval; entries flagged as unreasonable, duplicative, or not corresponding to delivered work may be rejected from credit.
  • Any additional materials, third-party costs, or rentals advanced by Owner on Contractor's behalf during the work-off period will be added to the balance unless otherwise agreed in writing.

6. Conditional Interest Waiver — Continuity Requirement

Interest is waived only so long as Contractor continues working steadily to discharge the balance.

  • So long as Contractor is performing logged, verifiable work toward the balance, no interest accrues and the previously-applicable rate (~7.95%, US Bank 2nd mortgage variable, per Agreement #2) is held in abeyance.
  • Stop-Work Trigger: If Contractor's logged work activity for the Owner ceases for any period greater than two (2) consecutive weeks, at any time prior to the balance being paid in full, the waiver is automatically and immediately revoked.
  • Retroactive Accrual: Upon revocation, interest at the Agreement #2 rate (~7.95% annualized, US Bank 2nd mortgage variable) becomes immediately due, calculated retroactively from May 17, 2025 (the date by which the original project was contractually required to have been completed) on the original $40,236.00 principal, less work credits at the dates they were earned.
  • Once retroactively triggered, interest continues to accrue prospectively on the then-remaining balance until paid in full.
  • Bona-fide medical, family-emergency, or weather-related interruptions communicated to Owner in writing at the time of the interruption may, at Owner's sole discretion, be excluded from the two-week stop-work calculation.
  • Full Waiver: Interest is permanently waived in its entirety only if Contractor works continuously (without any two-week-or-greater gap) until the entire balance is paid down to $0.
