4. Credits and Substitute-Labor Adjustments Since Agreement #2

How the $25,960 projection in §2 interacts with the §4 line items

The Sep 11, 2025 acknowledgment built the $40,236 balance using a projected $25,960 for "remaining cost to complete unfinished work" — Owner's estimate at that date of what it would cost to finish the contracted scope using Contractor's own per-task quoted rates plus materials. Since then, that projection has been progressively realized into actuals:

  • Realized as substitute-labor cash (§4.2): $12,114.45 has actually been spent on Donald Moden and Jackie Giroux for Justin-scope work. This portion of the projection is now real cash out the door.
  • Credited as Contractor work-off (§4.1 and §4.3): $2,790 of Sep–Nov 2025 bathroom credits + $11,631.00 of Feb–May 2026 tracked hours = $14,421.00 of Contractor labor credited against the balance. To the extent these hours substitute for what would otherwise have been hired out, they discharge the labor portion of the $25,960 projection.
  • Still unrealized: The remaining unfinished work — primarily blocked by current permitting issues — represents material costs Owner has not yet incurred and labor not yet performed. These remain inside the §2 principal until they materialize.

The §5 net balance therefore stacks actual substitute-labor outlay on top of the §2 principal (to capture real cash spent), then subtracts Contractor's work-off credits (to recognize hours actually delivered). The parties acknowledge there may be modest overlap between the original $25,960 projection and the $12,114 actually paid to Donald/Jackie; this will be reconciled jointly under §9.

7. Conditional Interest Waiver — Continuity Requirement

Interest is waived only so long as Contractor continues working steadily to discharge the balance.

  • So long as Contractor is performing logged, verifiable work toward the balance, no interest accrues and the interest rate set in §3 (7.95% annualized) is held in abeyance.
  • Stop-Work Trigger: If Contractor's logged work activity for the Owner ceases for any period greater than two (2) consecutive weeks, at any time prior to the balance being paid in full, the waiver is automatically and immediately revoked.
  • Abandonment — Civil and Criminal Exposure: If Contractor abandons the work-off effort prior to the balance being paid in full (i.e., any sustained cessation beyond the two-week Stop-Work Trigger without a bona-fide excuse documented under the carve-out below), Owner expressly reserves the right to pursue both civil claims and criminal charges arising out of Contractor's prior conduct — including, without limitation, claims and charges based on fraudulent misrepresentations and activities Contractor engaged in during the original project (false statements regarding work completed, materials purchased, payments received, scheduling commitments, and other representations made to induce Owner's payments under quotes #2516, #2517, #2522, #2524 et al.). Contractor's continuing performance under this Agreement is a material part of Owner's consideration for forbearing from pursuing such claims; abandonment terminates that forbearance. Nothing in this paragraph waives or limits any other remedy available to Owner at law or in equity.
