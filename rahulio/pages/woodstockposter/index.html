About the Bearsville Theater

  • Built from an 18th-century barn on 16 acres along Sawkill Creek, about two miles west of the Woodstock village green.
  • Conceived by Albert Grossman — manager of Bob Dylan, Janis Joplin and The Band — as an acoustically perfect room, designed by John Storyk, architect of Electric Lady Studios. Grossman died in 1986, before the theater opened in 1989; he is buried in the memorial park behind it.
  • Part of the Bearsville Center: Bearsville Studios hosted the Rolling Stones, R.E.M., Muddy Waters and The Pretenders; Utopia Studios was built in 1979 for Todd Rundgren.
  • Restored by Lizzie Vann in 2019; Peter Shapiro's Dayglo Presents took over in 2024 and installed a Meyer Sound system. Capacity: 500 standing, about 260 seated.
