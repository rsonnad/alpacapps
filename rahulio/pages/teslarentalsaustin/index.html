What the numbers include

How the all-in figures were built.

Driver-side add-ons

  • Tires: about $40/mo wherever the driver buys them.
  • Extra miles on 10k-mile leases: about $42/mo (2,000 miles a year at $0.25).
  • Upkeep and repairs when the driver owns the car: about $100–165/mo.
  • Tesla lease return fee: $395 at the end of the term.

Texas tax on leases

Texas charges its 6.25% motor vehicle tax once, on the car’s full price, and Tesla collects it at delivery. On a $27,500 used Model 3 that’s $1,718.75, plus $434 in registration, inspection and plate fees. The monthly payment itself isn’t taxed again. Tax on a new-car lease is an estimate (6.25% of about $42k).

The private rental’s hidden extra

In a private rental the owner pays for breakdowns, tires and wear, so the renter effectively gets a warranty and a maintenance plan. A Tesla lease includes the warranty but not tires or wear charges. Buying includes neither. The trade-off for the owner: on a 112k-mile car, a single drive-unit failure ($3–7k) costs more than a year of rent.
