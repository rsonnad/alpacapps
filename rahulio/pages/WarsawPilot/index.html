Warsaw Pilot — Product Brief

The Problem

Connected Everywhere, Belonging Nowhere

Modern professionals — especially in fast-growing cities — face a paradox: surrounded by people but deeply disconnected. They have colleagues but not community. Social media but not real relationships. They want to improve their health, grow their careers, and find meaningful connection — but they're doing it alone.

Existing solutions fall short:

  • Networking events are transactional. Business cards exchanged, never followed up.
  • Dating apps reduce people to profiles. No context, no trust, no organic chemistry.
  • Fitness classes build community around one axis but miss the others.
  • Meetups are inconsistent, poorly curated, and have high churn.
  • Mastermind groups are expensive, small, and career-only.

None of these learn. None get better over time. None connect the dots across health, career, and relationships simultaneously.

The Solution

60 People. Weekly Tea. AI That Learns.

Three Pillars is a curated community of 60 professionals who meet weekly for afternoon tea. An AI engine learns each member's goals, personality, and interaction patterns — then orchestrates connections that compound in value over time.

It's not networking. Not therapy. Not a dating event. It's a recurring, AI-enhanced gathering where the right people keep meeting the right people — and the AI gets smarter every session.

The Application

Prospective members answer five questions. Substantive, specific answers required for at least four.

Growth “What are you actively building or changing in your life right now?”
Health “What's one specific thing you're doing for your health?”
Wealth “What's your primary professional or financial goal for the next 12 months?”
Connection “What kind of relationship or connection is most missing from your life?”
Contribution “What can you offer this community? This could be a skill, knowledge, experience — or simply your willingness to show up for others and help them achieve their goals.”

Group Composition

  • 60 members per cohort
  • ~60% regulars (attend 3+ times/month), ~40% rotating
  • Gender balance: ~50/50
  • Professional diversity: minimum 8 industries represented
  • No more than 15% from any single profession
The Experience

Saturday Afternoon Tea

The Space

A warm, well-designed venue in central Warsaw with:

  • 6 long communal tables seating 10 each, with a dedicated tea server per table
  • 12–15 breakout nooks around the perimeter — small tables for 2, window seats, armchairs
  • A “Give Wall” near the entrance for member offers
  • Natural materials, warm lighting, plants, real ceramics
  • Background music curated for each phase of the afternoon
  • No screens, no projectors, no presentation slides

The Flow (2.5 Hours)

15 min
Arrival + Check-in
Members arrive, check in via app or at the door, receive their Evening Card. Hosts greet everyone by name with AI-fed context.
35 min
Round 1 — Table of 10
AI-curated seating. Each table has a volunteer tea server and a Table Card with a conversation prompt crafted for that specific group. Family-style tea and accompaniments.
15 min
Community Moment
Everyone stands. A brief whole-room segment that rotates weekly: wellness practice, skill shares, personal stories, or open micro-lessons. Always led by a member.
35 min
Round 2 — Table of 10
New table, new group, deeper prompt. By Round 2, people are warmed up and conversations go deeper. After two rounds: 18 unique tablemates.
30–40 min
Breakout Pairs
The most important segment. Members move to the nooks for focused 1:1 conversations with AI-selected partners. 15 minutes with the right person, with the right context, produces more value than 2 hours of mingling.
15 min
Open Social + Departure
Unstructured. Continue conversations, make new introductions, warm departures. The tea servers bring a final cup.

The Host Role

Two hosts per event. Not MCs — they're the human embodiment of the AI. Warm, socially skilled people who greet everyone by name, circulate during rounds, and make specific introductions during open social. A subtle earpiece feeds them context from the AI.

Post-Event

  • Quick rating: how was tonight?
  • Tap to request connections with tablemates or breakout partners
  • Double opt-in — both people must tap for contact info to be shared
  • Real phone numbers, not in-app messaging. The AI gets out of the way.

Between Events

The app is quiet — two touchpoints per week max:

  • Mid-week check-in: “Did you follow up with anyone from Saturday?”
  • Pre-event preview: “This Saturday's group has a nutritionist, two founders, and someone who just ran their first half-marathon. Coming?”

Outcome data (did the connection produce value?) is the training signal that makes the AI better every week.

Contribution Tracking

The AI measures and rewards contribution:

  • High contributors get the best matches. The most interesting new members and highest-value breakout pairings flow toward generous members.
  • Low contributors get a gentle nudge from hosts.
  • Persistent takers are counseled out via a quiet conversation. This protects the culture.
The Pilot

Why Warsaw

  • Demographic density: Enormous concentration of 28–48 year old professionals, many of whom moved to Warsaw from elsewhere in Poland
  • Cultural homogeneity: Shared language, education, values, and economic context creates a strong natural binding
  • The loneliness gap: Young professionals have colleagues but not community. Many left their social networks in their hometown.
  • Café culture: Afternoon tea is culturally native. Sitting for hours over herbata in a kawiarnia is normal.
  • Cost structure: Venue and tea service costs enable accessible pricing for the local professional class.
  • Bilingual potential: Warsaw's international community allows for Polish + English events, with AI routing speakers to compatible tables.

Scaling

One cohort = 60 members in one city. Scale by:

  1. Adding cohorts in Warsaw — different neighborhoods, age bands, or days
  2. Other Polish cities — Kraków, Wrocław, Gdańsk (same cultural binding)
  3. International expansion — each city gets its own cultural adaptation

The AI is the scalable asset. The local execution (venue, hosts, tea) is the moat.
