What We Do

Physical Spaces, Infused with AI

Three Pillars is a technology company that builds and operates physical spaces designed for human growth. Each space is a real property — land, buildings, gardens, animals — where an AI layer operates the environment, connects people, and helps members reach their goals.

Think of it as a country club for your soul, run by AI.

The AI doesn't live on a screen. It lives in the space. It controls the sauna temperature, schedules the cold plunge, manages the garden irrigation, runs the lighting and music, assigns seating at the weekly gathering of 64 members, and learns over time what each person needs to grow. The technology is invisible. The experience is a place that takes care of you.

The Technology Thesis

Most AI companies are building software. We're building the real-world interface — physical spaces where AI has agency over the environment and can directly impact human wellbeing. The insight is that AI's most transformative application isn't chatbots or image generation. It's operating the physical world around people in ways that make them healthier, wiser, more connected, and more grounded.

The Stack

Three Layers

Every Three Pillars space runs on the same architecture:

Physical Layer
The property. Land, buildings, gardens, animals, sauna, cold plunge, gym, gathering hall, quiet spaces, outdoor areas. Plus the hardware: smart lighting, climate controls, directional audio, displays, sensors (occupancy, temperature, humidity, CO2, soil moisture), electronic access, irrigation systems. Everything addressable by the AI.
AI Layer
The operator. Sensor fusion, contextual awareness, decision engine. The AI sees who's present, what's scheduled, what the weather is, what each member's goals are, and what happened last week. It translates all of this into physical actions: adjusting the sauna, watering the garden, assigning tables, cueing the hosts, shifting the lights. It learns from outcomes and gets better every week.
Experience Layer
What people feel. Members don't interact with the AI — they interact with the space, and the space is the AI. The sauna is ready when you arrive. The garden bed you tend is watered. The person seated across from you at Saturday's gathering has exactly the skill you need. Nobody touches a control. It just works.

What Members Achieve

Four Dimensions of Growth

Every member declares goals across four areas. The AI tracks progress and optimizes the entire space to serve them.

Health

Sauna protocols, cold plunge, gym, fresh food from the garden, movement outdoors. The AI tracks what you do and prescribes what's next. "You haven't done cold plunge in 2 weeks. Tuesday at 7 AM?"

Education

Workshops, skill-sharing, mentorship. The AI pairs you with people who know what you want to learn. It suggests sessions based on your goals and tracks whether you're growing.

Social

Weekly gatherings of 64. AI-matched seating. Shared meals. Working side by side in the garden. Relationships grounded in real shared experience, not networking small talk.

Spiritual

Quiet spaces for meditation. Hands in the soil. Animals. Fire pit under the stars. The AI protects your stillness — dim lights, locked doors, silence. It creates the conditions for inner work.

The Culture

A Place That Runs on Giving

Here's the insight: when a space provides genuine abundance — fresh food from the garden, a body heated by the sauna, real relationships, meaningful learning, spiritual grounding — scarcity dissolves. And when you're not in scarcity, the single greatest dopamine hit is giving something amazing to someone else. Watching their eyes light up. Knowing you made their day.

Three Pillars is built on this premise. The space takes care of your needs so thoroughly that your natural impulse shifts from "What can I get?" to "What can I give?" The AI doesn't force generosity. It creates the conditions where generosity is the most rewarding thing you can do — and then it makes giving effortless.

How the Space Encourages Giving

🌱 The Garden Is Shared Labor
Nobody owns a bed. Everyone works the same soil. You plant for the community, not yourself. When you eat the soup on Saturday, you're eating what other members grew. Giving is literal — you put food on someone else's table.
🎓 Members Teach Members
There are no hired instructors. Every workshop is taught by a member sharing what they know. The AI identifies who has knowledge others need and nudges them: "4 members want to learn fermentation. You've been doing it for years. Would you teach a session?"
🐔 Animal Care Rotates
Feeding the chickens, cleaning the coop, checking on the goats — these are shared duties. The AI assigns rotation, but the act itself is giving: you show up early so the animals are cared for, not because it benefits you directly.
🤝 AI Rewards Givers
The AI tracks contribution — who teaches, who tends the garden, who mentors, who shows up for others. High contributors get the best matches at Saturday gatherings. The most interesting new members flow toward generous people. Giving is rewarded with connection.

The Contribution Score

The AI maintains an invisible contribution score for every member. It's never displayed as a number — no leaderboards, no gamification. But it shapes the experience:

  • High contributors get priority breakout pairings, first access to new members, and are chosen for workshop teaching opportunities
  • Steady contributors get the full experience — this is most members
  • Low contributors get gentle nudges from the AI: "The garden could use help Wednesday. Join Marta and Tomek?" It offers specific, low-friction ways to give.
  • Persistent takers get a quiet conversation from a host. Not a punishment — an invitation to shift. If it doesn't change, they're counseled out to protect the culture.

Built Into the Gathering

The Saturday event itself is structured around giving:

  • Table prompts are outward-focused: "What's something you could help someone at this table with?" not "What do you need?"
  • The Give Wall: A physical board near the entrance where members post offers — "I have extra seedlings," "I can help with your business plan," "Free headshots this week"
  • Connection requests are about helping: When the AI suggests someone to meet, it frames it as mutual: "You can help her with X. She can help you with Y."
  • The communal meal: Eating food you collectively grew is the most primal form of giving. Every Saturday, generosity is on the table — literally.
In a world of sustainable abundance, your greatest high is giving something incredible to someone else. The space creates the abundance. The AI makes the giving effortless.

Feedback Loop

Measuring What Matters

The AI doesn't guess whether the space is working. It measures growth across all four dimensions and adapts.

After Every Visit

  • How was today? — 1–5, one tap
  • What you did: Sauna / Garden / Gym / Workshop / Gathering / Quiet time
  • Connections: Tap to request contact from anyone you met (double opt-in)
  • How you feel leaving: Energized / Calm / Inspired / Connected / Grounded

Monthly Growth Check

  • Health: "Is your body better off because of Three Pillars?" — 1–5
  • Education: "Did you learn something meaningful?" — 1–5
  • Social: "Did you form or deepen a real relationship?" — 1–5
  • Spiritual: "Did you find stillness, meaning, or grounding?" — 1–5
  • Referral: "Would you invite someone you love to join?" — Already have / Yes / No

Why Now

The Moment

Three things are converging:

  • AI is ready for the physical world. Sensor costs are down 90% in a decade. LLMs can reason about context. Edge compute can run decision engines locally. The technology to operate a physical space autonomously exists today.
  • People are starving for real places. Post-pandemic, the hunger for physical community, nature, and embodied experience is at an all-time high. Digital everything has left people disconnected from their bodies, from the land, and from each other.
  • Poland is the right launchpad. Exceptional construction quality at 40–60% of Western costs. Top-5 European tech talent. Land accessible within 30–60 min of Warsaw. Strong sauna culture, agricultural heritage, and community hunger among young professionals.
Every AI company is building for screens. We're building for soil, water, fire, and the people who gather around them.
