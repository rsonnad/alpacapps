Three Pillars

A country club for your soul, run by AI.

A wellness compound outside Warsaw with saunas, cold plunge, gardens, animals, gym, education, and weekly gatherings of 64 — all operated by an AI that learns what you need and shapes the environment around you.

Warsaw Pilot — Business Plan

The Idea

Everything You Need to Grow, in One Place

A country club gives you access to a property, facilities, and a community. But traditional country clubs are about status and golf. Three Pillars is about becoming a healthier, wiser, more connected version of yourself.

The compound has everything: saunas and cold plunge for your body. Gardens and animals for grounding. A gym for strength. Workshops and mentorship for your mind. Gathering spaces for community. Quiet zones for your spirit.

And instead of a massive staff running all of it, an AI operates the entire property. It controls the sauna temperatures. It manages the garden irrigation. It schedules the cold plunge cycles. It runs the lighting and music. It orchestrates the weekly gathering of 64 members. It learns what each person needs and adapts the environment to serve them.

You don't use an app. You show up, and the place takes care of you.

The Property

What's on the Land

The compound is a single property — ideally 1–3 hectares outside Warsaw with a mix of buildings, outdoor space, and working land.

🧖 Sauna & Cold Plunge
Finnish-style sauna, infrared room, and cold plunge pool. Multiple temperature zones for contrast therapy. Outdoor cooling area between sessions.
AI manages: Temperature cycles, session timing, ventilation, lighting mood, occupancy limits
💪 Gym & Movement
Functional fitness equipment, free weights, yoga/stretching area. Outdoor training zone. Not a commercial gym — curated for the compound's members.
AI manages: Lighting and music for workout intensity, equipment scheduling, class coordination
🌱 Gardens
Vegetable beds, herb gardens, fruit trees, flower beds. Members work the land with their hands. Harvest feeds the kitchen. Seasonal rhythms ground the community.
AI manages: Irrigation scheduling, soil moisture sensors, planting calendar, harvest tracking, task assignments
🐔 Animals
Chickens, possibly goats. Fresh eggs for the kitchen. The rhythms of caring for animals ground people in something real. Kids love it too.
AI manages: Feeding schedules, coop climate, health monitoring sensors, care rotation among members
🏠 Gathering Hall
Main indoor space for the weekly 64-person gathering. 8 tables of 8. Stage area. Kitchen for communal meals. Warm materials, natural light, fireplace.
AI manages: Seating assignments, table displays, lighting shifts, music, event flow, transition cues
📚 Workshop & Learning
Space for classes, skill-sharing, and mentorship. Chalkboard, projector, workbenches. Members teach what they know. Guest instructors invited by the community.
AI manages: Scheduling, topic matching (pairs teachers with interested members), equipment setup
🔥 Outdoor Spaces
Fire pit, walking trails, meditation spots, outdoor dining. A place to be outside — alone or together. Seasonal use year-round.
AI manages: Outdoor lighting, fire pit ignition, trail wayfinding, weather-based scheduling
🧘 Quiet Spaces
Small rooms for meditation, breathwork, journaling, or prayer. No screens, no speakers. Warm light, natural materials. A place to go inward.
AI manages: Gentle lighting, do-not-disturb signals, booking, ambient temperature
The AI

How AI Lives in the Compound

The AI doesn't have a body. So the compound becomes its body. Every sensor, speaker, light, display, valve, and lock is a limb. The AI expresses itself through five channels:

🖥
Screens
Table displays, entry boards, workshop schedules. The AI's voice in the room — showing who's here, what's happening, and who you should meet.
🎧
Host Earpieces
Two humans with earpieces are the AI's hands. It whispers context and suggestions. The host makes the introduction. The AI orchestrates, the human delivers.
📱
Phones
Private channel to each member. During events: "The person across from you raises bees — you mentioned wanting to start." Between visits: "The garden needs weeding Thursday. Join?"
⚙️
The Facilities
The sauna knows your preferred temperature. The cold plunge is ready when you arrive. The garden bed you tend is watered. The tools anticipate because the AI knows your patterns.
🌤
The Environment
Lighting shifts to match the time of day and what's happening. Music sets mood without anyone choosing it. Temperature adapts to occupancy. The space nudges without words.
🌾
The Land
Soil sensors, weather data, irrigation. The AI manages the garden and animals so the land thrives — but members do the hands-on work. The AI coordinates, nature produces.
People don't interact with the AI. They interact with the space, and the space is the AI.
Member Goals

What People Come Here For

Every member has goals across four dimensions. The AI knows them and optimizes the entire compound to serve them.

Health

Sauna and cold plunge protocols. Gym routines. Fresh food from the garden. Movement outdoors. The AI tracks what you do and suggests what's next — "You haven't done cold plunge in 2 weeks. Tuesday morning slot is open."

Education

Workshops, skill-sharing, mentorship. The AI pairs you with people who know what you want to learn. It suggests workshops based on your goals. It tracks your growth — not with tests, but with the question: "Are you learning what you came here to learn?"

Social

Weekly gatherings of 64. AI-matched seating. Shared meals. Working side by side in the garden. The relationships that form here are grounded in real shared experience — not networking small talk.

Spiritual

Quiet spaces for meditation and reflection. Being with animals. Hands in the soil. Fire pit conversations under the stars. The AI creates space for this — literally. Dim lights, locked doors, silence. It protects your stillness.

Feedback Loop

How We Know It's Working

The AI measures whether the compound is actually helping people grow — across all four dimensions.

After Every Visit

A 60-second check-in on the member's phone:

  • Overall: "How was today?" — 1–5, one tap
  • What you did: Sauna / Garden / Gym / Workshop / Gathering / Quiet time / Other
  • Connection: "Did you meet someone valuable?" — tap to request contact (double opt-in)
  • Feeling: "How do you feel leaving?" — Energized / Calm / Inspired / Connected / Tired

Monthly Value Check

A deeper reflection, 3–5 minutes, once a month:

  • Health: "Is your body better off this month because of Three Pillars?" — 1–5
  • Education: "Did you learn something meaningful?" — 1–5
  • Social: "Did you form or deepen a real relationship?" — 1–5
  • Spiritual: "Did you find stillness, meaning, or grounding here?" — 1–5
  • Referral: "Would you invite someone you love to join?" — Already have / Yes / Maybe / No

What the AI Does with Feedback

SignalAI Response

The Pilot

Why Poland

  • Land cost: 1–3 hectares within 30–60 minutes of Warsaw is achievable. Property prices are a fraction of Western Europe.
  • Construction quality: Polish craftsmanship is exceptional. Fit-out costs 40–60% lower than the US or UK.
  • Sauna culture: Deeply rooted. Poles understand contrast therapy, banya traditions, and the social ritual of heat and cold.
  • Agricultural heritage: Gardening, animal husbandry, and connection to the land are part of the cultural DNA, not a hipster trend.
  • Tech talent: Top-5 European tech hub. Engineers who can build IoT systems, AI pipelines, and sensor networks.
  • Community hunger: Young professionals in Warsaw are actively seeking something deeper than nightlife and networking events.

Next Steps

Path to Opening

  1. Find the land — scout properties 30–60 min from Warsaw. 1–3 hectares, existing structures preferred. Agritourism zoning.
  2. Design the compound — architect + landscape designer + IoT engineer. Plan buildings, gardens, animal areas, and infrastructure together.
  3. Build the sauna & plunge first — this is the anchor. People will come for this alone while the rest of the compound develops.
  4. Plant the gardens — seasonal. Start with easy wins: herbs, tomatoes, greens. First harvest within 3 months. Expand each season.
  5. Build the AI operator — start simple: sauna controls, lighting, scheduling. Add matching engine, feedback loops, and garden management over time.
  6. Recruit 30 founding members — referral-based. People who want to sweat, grow food, learn, and connect. Not just attend — participate.
  7. Open & grow — first month is gathering data. By month 3, the AI knows the members. By month 6, the garden is producing. By year 1, the compound feels like it's been there forever.
