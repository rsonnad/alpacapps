The Concept

A Space That Runs Itself

Three Pillars is a physical venue — part creative studio, part fabrication lab, part networking hub — where an AI operates everything. The lighting. The music. The video rooms. The 3D printers. The cameras. The flow of people through the space.

Members don't learn an app or configure settings. They walk in and the space responds. The AI knows the schedule, senses who's present, and orchestrates the environment in real time. When 64 people arrive for the weekly networking event, the AI doesn't just set the mood — it runs the event. It assigns seating, sequences introductions, controls what's on the screens, and adjusts the room as energy shifts.

The result is a space that feels like it has a brilliant, invisible host — one who also happens to run a fabrication lab and a photography studio.

The Three Pillars

Space, Tools, People

The Space
AI-controlled environment. Smart lighting per zone (color temperature, intensity, circadian rhythm). Directional audio — different music in the main hall vs. the maker lab vs. breakout rooms. Climate that adapts to occupancy. Video walls and displays that the AI programs. Motorized shading. Electronic access control. The AI is the building manager.
The Tools
Creative and fabrication infrastructure. 3D printers and 3D sign-making equipment for physical prototyping and production. A photography studio with professional lighting, cameras, and backdrops — all AI-controllable. Video rooms for recording, streaming, and conferencing. The AI manages queues, presets, and calibration.
The People
AI-orchestrated networking. Groups of 64 meet weekly. The AI assigns seating, generates conversation prompts tailored to each table, sequences rounds, and manages the flow between structured networking and open social time. It learns who connects well and gets smarter every week.

The Event

64 People, Every Week, Run by AI

The signature event is a weekly gathering of 64 members. The AI doesn't just set the mood — it orchestrates the entire experience.

Scenario • Weekly Networking • Saturday 3:00 PM
64 members arrive for a 2.5-hour AI-orchestrated event
2:00 PM
Space prep. AI wakes the venue. Main hall lights to warm 2700K. Background music begins. HVAC pre-cools to 21°C (will rise with body heat). Displays show today's seating assignments and featured member profiles. Maker lab and studios go to standby. autonomous
3:00 PM
Check-in. Members badge in at the door. The AI knows who's arrived, updates seating in real time if someone is missing, and displays each person's table assignment on the entry screen. Hosts get a live feed via earpiece: who just arrived, any context worth knowing. reactive
3:15 PM
Round 1 — Tables of 8. AI has assigned 8 tables based on member profiles, goals, and past interactions. Each table's display shows a conversation prompt crafted for that specific mix of people. Audio zones keep tables distinct. Music fades to background level. orchestrated
3:55 PM
Transition cue. Lights pulse gently. Music shifts. Displays update with Round 2 assignments — entirely new tables, new people. AI compensates for anyone who left early. event-driven
4:00 PM
Round 2 — New tables of 8. Different group, deeper prompt. After two rounds, each person has sat with 14 unique people. The AI tracks which pairings generated energy (via conversation duration, body language, follow-up requests). learning
4:40 PM
Community spotlight. Main hall lights brighten. A member shares a 5-minute story, demo, or skill. Screens display supporting visuals. Audio switches to voice-optimized EQ. The AI chose this person based on what they're building and what the group would value. curated
4:50 PM
Open networking. Structure dissolves. The AI suggests 3 specific people to meet via each member's phone — based on everything it learned in Rounds 1 and 2. Breakout nooks light up. Photo studio opens for headshots. Maker lab available for demos. suggestive
5:30 PM
Wind-down. Lights shift cooler. Music signals closure. Displays show a recap: who was here, key connections made, next week's preview. As people leave, zones go to standby one by one. Last person out — AI locks up and logs the session. autonomous

Between Events

The space is open to members all week for:

  • Fabrication: Use the 3D printers, sign makers, and CNC equipment. AI manages booking and queues.
  • Photography: Book the studio. AI sets lighting presets for your shoot type.
  • Video: Record content, take calls, stream. Soundproofed rooms, AI-managed displays.
  • Focused work: Breakout nooks with AI-optimized lighting and climate for deep work.
  • Small gatherings: Host your own meetup in the main hall. AI adapts the environment to your group size.
Feedback Loop

How We Know It's Working

The AI can only improve if it knows what's producing value. Three feedback layers — immediate, weekly, and longitudinal — give it the signal it needs.

1. Post-Event Pulse (Immediate)

Within 30 minutes of each event, every attendee gets a 60-second check-in on their phone:

Post-Event Pulse
  • Overall: “How was today?” — 1–5 scale, one tap
  • Connections: “Did you meet someone valuable today?” — Yes / Not yet / No
  • Highlight: “Best part?” — tap one: Table Round / Spotlight / Breakout / Tools / Open Social
  • Connect: Tap to request contact info from anyone at your tables — double opt-in

Takes under a minute. High completion rate because it's immediate and effortless. This is the primary training signal for the matching AI.

2. Mid-Week Follow-Up (3 Days Later)

A single-question check-in, delivered Tuesday or Wednesday:

Mid-Week Check-In
  • Follow-up: “Did anything come from Saturday's connections?” — Yes (tell us) / Not yet / Didn't follow up
  • Outcome type: If yes: Coffee/meeting / Collaboration / Referral / Friendship / Business deal / Other

This is where the AI learns which connections actually produced value — the difference between a pleasant conversation and a relationship that matters.

3. Monthly Value Check (Longitudinal)

Once a month, a slightly deeper reflection. 3–5 minutes:

Monthly Value Assessment
  • Net value: “Is Three Pillars worth more to you now than when you joined?” — Much more / Somewhat more / About the same / Less
  • Pillar check: Rate your progress this month in: Connections made / Skills learned / Things built — 1–5 each
  • Tools usage: Which tools did you use? (3D printers / Sign maker / Photo studio / Video rooms / None)
  • Referral signal: “Would you invite a friend to join?” — Already have / Yes / Maybe / No
  • Open: “What would make this more valuable to you?” — free text, optional

The AI doesn't guess whether it's working. It asks, measures, and adapts — every week.
Design Principles

How We Build

01
AI Operates, Humans Override
The AI runs the space by default. Humans can always override — but they rarely need to. The best compliment: nobody notices the technology.
02
Make Things, Not Just Talk
The fabrication tools are the point. 3D printers, cameras, sign makers. People come here to produce something physical, not just attend meetings.
03
64, Not 640
Intimate scale. 64 is enough for serendipity but small enough that the AI can learn every person. Two rounds of 8 = 14 unique connections per event.
04
Every Device on the Network
If the AI can't reach it, it can't control it. Every light, speaker, printer, camera, lock, and display is addressable. No dead zones.
05
Warm Materials, Hidden Tech
Wood, textiles, ceramics, plants. The technology disappears into the walls. The space feels like a home run by a thoughtful person, not a tech showroom. The AI is felt, not seen.
The Pilot

Why Warsaw

  • Build cost: Fit-out and renovation 40–60% cheaper than Western Europe or the US. Excellent craftsmanship.
  • Tech talent: Top-5 European tech hub. Engineers who can wire IoT, build AI pipelines, and integrate hardware.
  • Maker culture: Growing demand for fabrication access — 3D printing, CNC, laser cutting — but few quality shared spaces.
  • Community hunger: Young professionals who moved to Warsaw crave belonging and creative outlets beyond their day jobs.
  • Real estate: 350 m² in central Warsaw is achievable without raising millions.

Next Steps

Path to Opening

  1. Find the space — scout commercial venues in Śródmieście, Mokotów, or Praga. 300–400 m², high ceilings, good bones, ground floor.
  2. Design the layout — architect + IoT engineer plan the zones. Main hall for 64, maker lab, photo studio, video rooms, breakout nooks. Every surface wired.
  3. Procure equipment — 3D printers, CNC/laser cutter, photo lighting, cameras, audio, displays. Source locally where possible.
  4. Build the AI operator — sensor integration, hardware control APIs, event orchestration engine. Start with lighting + audio + displays. Add matching over time.
  5. Renovate & install — 10–14 week build-out. Construction and hardware installation in parallel with software.
  6. Recruit 30 founding members — referral-based. Makers, creators, professionals. People who will use the tools and give honest feedback.
  7. Launch weekly events — first month is data collection. AI learns the space, the people, the patterns. By month 3 it should feel alive.
