The capability gap

Your engagement scores are a symptom. The room is the cause.

People are disconnected, distracted and performing. In an AI-shaped workplace, the ability to elevate human energy and emotion becomes one of the few skills that can't be automated — and it's the one almost no leadership curriculum teaches.

Meetings where nobody moves first

The same three extroverts carry every discussion. Everyone else waits out the clock. Participation isn't a personality trait — it's a condition a skilled leader creates in the first ninety seconds.

Hybrid teams that never quite connect

Distributed work removed the corridor, the coffee queue and the accidental conversation. What's left has to be deliberately designed — and most managers were never shown how.

Offsites that fade by Wednesday

A great away-day generates a week of good feeling and no behaviour change. Without repeatable skills and follow-through, the investment evaporates before the next quarter.

The difference

Conventional leadership training stops where this starts

Traditional programs teach people to manage responsibilities, make decisions and hit goals. They rarely teach how to elevate human energy, emotion and psychological safety.

  • Ends when the session ends

Outcomes

What changes, and how you'll know

How we measure it

Before

A short baseline pulse on participation, psychological safety and manager connection, plus the relevant items from your existing engagement survey.

During

Facilitator observation against defined behaviours — who initiates, who is drawn in, how quickly a group moves from silence to contribution.

Day 30

Self-report plus a manager check: which mechanics are actually in use, in which meetings, and what got in the way.

Day 90

Repeat pulse against baseline, delivered as a short written read-out for your people team with recommendations for the next cohort.

Questions your team will ask

Is this a wellbeing perk or actual leadership development?

It's a leadership program. Wellbeing sessions ask people to feel better; this one teaches a specific, observable capability — how to raise the energy, safety and participation of a group — and then makes participants practise it under coaching until it's repeatable.

The distinction matters at budget time: this maps to manager effectiveness and engagement, not to a perks line.

Our leaders are senior. Is this too basic for them?

No. Senior leaders have usually had extensive training in decisions, strategy and performance management, and almost none in elevating human energy and emotion. That's the gap this fills. Sessions are calibrated to the seniority in the room.

Half our team are introverts. Will they hate it?

This is the most common concern, and the honest answer is that quiet participants often get the most from it. Nothing here requires being funny, loud or performative. Presence is taught as a set of mechanics — how you open, how you respond, how you frame a prompt — and mechanics are learnable by anyone.

Practice happens in pairs and small groups before anything happens in front of the full room.

Can you deliver virtually, and across regions?

Yes. Keynotes and half-day workshops run virtually with adapted exercises for video. For multi-region rollouts, the facilitator certification cohort is usually the better economics: we train and certify your internal facilitators, and they deliver locally with our materials and ongoing support.

What do you need from us?

A named sponsor in L&D or People, a room that can be rearranged (chairs that move matter more than AV), a confirmed participant list two weeks out, and permission to run a five-minute baseline pulse beforehand. For offsites, we'll want a call with whoever owns the agenda.

How is it priced?

Engagements are scoped per proposal, based on format, group size, number of facilitators, travel and whether reinforcement is included. You'll get a fixed written price before anything is committed.

For individual leaders, open-enrolment seats on the three-day hosted intensive are $1,200 USD per person, all-inclusive of accommodation, meals and materials — a practical way to trial the curriculum with one or two people before committing a cohort.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. If the engagement doesn't deliver what the proposal set out, we'll run an equivalent session for your team at no additional fee. Specific terms are written into each statement of work.

How far ahead do we need to book?

Keynotes and workshops typically need four to six weeks. Multi-day intensives and offsites need eight to twelve, mostly for venue and participant scheduling. Certification cohorts are scheduled by quarter.
