Mitigation: emphasize the $30K in physical assets (office, equipment), $15K in hiring, and the management arm (clearly physical work). The business plan must frame technology as the competitive advantage, not the entire business.
📚 Mitigation Strategy Structure the E-2 application to lead with the property management arm (8-12% management fees, physical on-site work, employee oversight). Present the technology platform as the proprietary tool that enables competitive advantage. This reframes the narrative from "tech company with no infrastructure costs" to "property management company with proprietary AI technology."

Key Success Factors

🎯

Lead with Management

Frame the E-2 application around property management (physical, tangible) with technology as the competitive advantage -- not the other way around.

👥

Hire Early

Start with 2 part-time employees in Month 1. Job creation is a critical E-2 factor. Even part-time W-2 employees demonstrate commitment.

💰

Spend Irrevocably

The investment must be "at risk." Sign the office lease, buy equipment, pay employees. Do not keep $80K in the bank as a "reserve."

📈

Document Everything

Business plan, financial projections, receipts, contracts, payroll records. E-2 applications are document-heavy. Over-prepare.

🚀

Revenue Before Filing

If possible, start generating revenue before the E-2 application. Even $2K/mo in management fees shows the business is real, not speculative.

🌎

Plan for Renewal

E-2 visas renew every 2-5 years. Year 2 revenue ($187K) and 5 employees provide a strong renewal case. Build toward these milestones deliberately.
