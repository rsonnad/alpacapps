What Immigration Looks For

Under INA Section 214(b), you must prove you will return to Thailand

💼 Economic Ties

  • A business you operate
  • Employment or clients
  • Ongoing income sources

👨‍👩‍👧 Social Ties

  • Family in Thailand
  • Community connections
  • Responsibilities at home

🏦 Financial Stability

  • Bank statements
  • Business records
  • Ability to fund travel

🎯 Clear Trip Purpose

  • Tourism itinerary
  • Business meetings
  • Market exploration
💡
They don't require extreme wealth — just evidence that you have a life, income, and reasons to return to Thailand.
💡

Why a Small Business Helps

Demonstrates ongoing obligations in Thailand

For a young applicant without property, a business helps demonstrate ties even without large capital.

  • Shows an active income source in Thailand
  • Shows a compelling reason to return
  • Shows initiative, credibility, and documentation
  • Provides concrete evidence for the interview
✅
US consulates do not require large capital. Many small online businesses globally start with $200–$500. Even a very small business can demonstrate ties if it is legitimate and active.

Sole Proprietorship Registration

กิจการเจ้าของคนเดียว — the simplest business structure in Thailand

1

Choose a Business Name

Example: "Chiang Mai Thai Wellness Shop"

2

Register with DBD

Department of Business Development — DBD Biz Regist Online System

Documents: Thai ID card, address, business description • Time: 1–3 days • Fee: THB 20–50 (~$1–2)
3

Tax ID Registration

Register with Thai Revenue Department

Revenue under THB 1.8M/year = No VAT registration required
4

Open a Bank Account (Optional)

Shows business activity and revenue flow

📋
Minimum capital requirement: None. A sole proprietorship requires very little capital and minimal paperwork.

Visa Interview Guide

The interview typically lasts 1–3 minutes. Keep answers short.

💬 Example Questions & Answers

🎙️ Why do you want to go to the United States?
I would like to visit for about two weeks for tourism. I also run a small Thai wellness products shop in Chiang Mai, so I'm interested in seeing spa and wellness stores while visiting a friend in Texas.
🎙️ What do you do for work?
I run a small online business in Chiang Mai selling Thai massage oils and herbal wellness kits. I started it earlier this year and sell mostly through Etsy and social media.
🎙️ How long will you stay?
About two weeks.
🎙️ Where will you stay?
I will stay with a friend in Austin, Texas for part of the trip and explore the area.
🎙️ Who will pay for the trip?
I will pay for most of the trip myself from my savings and business income.
🎙️ What will happen to your business while you travel?
My business is online so I can manage orders remotely, and a friend helps with shipping while I'm away.
🎙️ Do you plan to work in the US?
No. I will only visit for tourism and to learn about the wellness market.
🎙️ When will you return to Thailand?
After about two weeks. I need to return to continue running my business.

✅ Do

  • Answer in 1–2 sentences
  • Be calm and friendly
  • Be confident and brief
  • Stop talking after answering

🚫 Don't Say

  • "I want to work in the US"
  • "I might stay longer"
  • "I don't know how long"
  • Over-explain or ramble
