Acoustic Reckoning · 29 hidden songs

The easiest gains are the comb on the theater stairs, the pocket watch set to five, and a faded crimson ribbon threaded through the roses. All three sharpen a clue while preserving the quiet foreground.

These are art-direction options for the Weathered Oil family. The sources are published lyrics. The individual Spotify performances, covers, and live verse orders were not independently transcribed. Suggested objects and placements are interpretations.

  1. Brokedown Palace
    There are already ruins beside the water. Plant one young weeping willow at the waterline; the song ends with an act of planting. Lyrics
  2. Stella Blue
    The guitars establish the setting. Paint a broken-wing angel in the archtop’s reflection, or give a single rusty string a polished glint. Lyrics
  3. Dire Wolf
    Keep the wolf. Show two identical queens of spades where the deck fans out, the giveaway in the wolf’s impossible card game. Lyrics
  4. Oh Babe It Ain’t No Lie
    Keep the globe. Put a tiny whispering profile in its brass stand for the woman spreading stories about the returning traveler. Lyrics
  5. Monkey and the Engineer
    The little locomotive already lives in the left margin. Give it engine number 99 and a monkey’s hand on the throttle. Lyrics
  6. Been All Around This World
    Give this song the distant Blue Ridge ridgeline and a half-open door for Lulu. That frees the globe for Oh Babe. Lyrics
  7. Dark Hollow
    Let a pair of freight rails curve into the unlit gap between two pines. The train is the narrator’s way out. Lyrics
  8. Jack-A-Roe
    A playing-card jack misses the heroine’s story. Hide a tailor’s shears and a sailor’s jacket beside a tiny ship on a handbill. Lyrics
  9. To Lay Me Down
    Nest four or five glistening clover heads under the lower lettering. The lovers imagine resting in sparkling clover. Lyrics
  10. Ripple
    Keep the still pond and its unexplained rings. An empty tin cup on the bank adds a second clue from the invitation to reach out. Lyrics
  11. Cassidy
    The wolf already belongs to Dire Wolf. Slip a Cadillac’s tail fins between the distant trees, or hang a tambourine by the cradle. Lyrics
  12. Deep Elem Blues
    Use the shoe and money motif. Tuck the folded bill inside the shoe with just one corner visible, exactly where the narrator advises keeping it. Lyrics
  13. Friend of the Devil
    Give the fugitive a trail of hound prints ending at a little cave. This leaves the loose cash available for Deep Elem. Lyrics
  14. Sugaree
    Sugar cane is a title pun. Hide the silhouette of the approaching wagon on a handbill, with rain streaking its window. Lyrics
  15. Loser
    Make one foreground card the queen of diamonds and put a cold coffee cup beside it. Keep this red queen away from the wolf’s black queens. Lyrics
  16. Bird Song
    Move the small bird into flight, leaving an empty perch. The song’s emotional turn is that the singer was here briefly and has flown on. Lyrics
  17. High Time
    Work a broken muddy wheel and a few strands of hay into the woodland floor. They tell the hard-luck half of the song at thumbnail size. Lyrics
  18. Althea
    Hide a jester’s cap on a grave marker among the ruins. A tiny printed handbill could carry the same image without darkening the concert scene. Lyrics
  19. Row Jimmy
    The rabbit beside the rowboat is already a two-part clue. Keep the pair together; a half-dollar on the gunwale would give listeners a third way in. Lyrics
  20. Bertha
    Drape a green coat on a windswept branch. Pair it with a wet path leading to a dry doorway for the runner sheltering from rain. Lyrics
  21. Uncle John’s Band
    Set a violin in the mouth of a silver mine, using the existing ruins as the opening. The banjo can stay as a general acoustic detail. Lyrics
  22. Morning Dew
    Leave the tiny cradle visibly empty. A cradle turned toward a deserted path carries the song’s uncertainty about the crying child. Lyrics
  23. Brown-Eyed Women
    Stand a dusty bottle with a clear ruby gleam beside two sticks of hickory. Both the family’s liquor and the wood used to fire the still are in the verses. Lyrics
  24. He’s Gone
    Put a small cat on a patch of tin roofing. One readable silhouette gives the theater a lyric clue without adding a second train. Lyrics
  25. China Doll
    Set the existing pocket watch to five. Pair it with a tiny porcelain shard or a painted bell to connect the opening time to the fragile doll. Lyrics
  26. Peggy-O
    Place a gold hair comb on the new staircase. Peggy comes down the stairs combing her hair as she says farewell to William. Lyrics
  27. Black Peter
    The existing bed can carry this song. Set a fever thermometer reading 105 beside it and give the sickbed two visiting chairs. Lyrics
  28. It Must Have Been the Roses
    Thread a single faded crimson hair ribbon through the lower roses. It tells Annie’s story and naturally ties the softer billing into the border. Lyrics
  29. St. Stephen
    Build a miniature wishing well into the stonework with a gold bell above it and a bucket disappearing into shadow. These details belong together in the verse. Lyrics
