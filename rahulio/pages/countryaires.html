Notes on the per-capita picture

Small hubs leadSmall, high-income financial/tech hubs stand out sharply: Hong Kong, Singapore, and Switzerland show the highest density of both millionaires and (especially) billionaires relative to population.
The US and AustraliaThe United States and Australia remain very strong on millionaires per capita; the U.S. also leads large countries on billionaires per capita.
Punching above their weightGermany, Sweden, Taiwan, and Norway punch above their weight on billionaires per million people relative to many peers with similar or higher millionaire counts.
The scale effectLarge-population countries (China, India, Brazil, Mexico, Russia) have very low rates on both metrics, as expected given their scale.

Figures are approximate (population rounding and slight timing differences between millionaire and billionaire datasets). Exact per-capita rates will shift with updated population or wealth counts.
