House Rules

A few critical requests to keep the magic alive

Rule 01
Always Keep the Back Fence Gates Closed
Don't leave them open for a second or the Alpaca will wander off, and you may spend the rest of your life chasing lost alpaca dreams.
Rule 02
Parking: Respect Neighborhood Sovereignty
  • DO NOT park in front of neighbors' houses.
  • DO NOT park on the grass.
  • When you come in the gate, you can park in the gravel lot on the right. Park facing either the street or the container — and near other cars. We are not a hog playhouse.
  • If the lot is full, park across the street to the left past two houses, facing the highway (NOT in front of a house). Or park on the frontage road (turn left before the highway) to the right of the "no parking" signs. This is a 5 minute walk.
  • DO NOT PARK OR DRIVE ON THE GRASS beyond the gate. Sprinklers may get damaged, and you're likely to get stuck if it's rained. It's a $200 towing bill.
  • Don't park on the circle or by the garage — this blocks Amazon delivery access.
  • Don't park in front of the sauna or garage no matter how tempting.
Rule 03
Clean Up & Leave No Mess
Please be extra mindful to help with cleaning. Don't leave bags or items on counters in the Living Room or Kitchen — stash them in the dining room. Don't leave beverage cans, food items, or random stuff in the living room area. If you can't find something, check the "Scolding Mess Box" out back. Also: don't put anything in toilets other than toilet paper.
Rule 04
Figure Things Out On Your Own
Read the labels. Read the website. Try to guess where things are before asking someone. We believe in self-reliance at the Playhouse.
Rule 05
Dogs & Pets
Don't let your dog enter the Alpaca domain in the back, even though he/she is surely the most divine creature on Earth. You can tie them in the front. Don't leave pets unattended anywhere on the property if they are not tied. Don't bring animals into the house.
Rule 06
Bring a Reusable Water Bottle
There are 3 filtered water dispensers onsite. Don't bring full plastic water bottles. There are lots of bottles to borrow above the oven if you need one.
Rule 07
Do NOT Call the Alpaca "Llamas"
They look sweet, but if they hear you say "Llama," they will likely pull a knife and take you out — quick, silent, and drag you behind the barn. You've been warned.
