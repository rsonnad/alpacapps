About Marc Nathan

Senior Director of Market Development at Michael Best, a full-service law firm with a focus on venture-scale startups and growing enterprises. Marc brings qualified clients into the firm and helps them with fundraising strategy by connecting them to investors.

Marc is a "Super Connector" who hosts/sponsors several monthly meetups including OpenCoffee Club Austin and Wake Up! CPG. He's founded or moderates TexasCPG.org, AustinActiveNetworking.com, BoostATX, and several other active communities. Originally from Houston, he's lived in Austin since 2014.

His ask: Warm referrals to qualified technology-based and CPG startups that need legal representation and fundraising support in the Seed to Series B range.
