Bottom-Line View

A $409K offer is aggressive but defensible if framed around recent market evidence instead of worst-case rhetoric. It sits about 5% below the county's 2025 market value, pays a premium over the recent 4.66-acre $349,999 sale, and still leaves room for manufactured-home title, OSSF, insurance, and property-upgrade risk.

Seller-facing framing: this is not a claim that the property is impaired. It is a clean, financeable offer number that recognizes acreage value while reserving for diligence items that a conventional buyer and lender still have to clear.

Land, Rules & Future Use

Regulatory Context

JurisdictionUnincorporated Bastrop County unless a city/ETJ layer says otherwise.
ZoningBastrop County says it does not have general zoning authority in unincorporated areas.
Building permits / COCounty says it does not issue general building permits or Certificates of Occupancy; residential construction rules still apply in specified cases.
Development permitRequired for all development in unincorporated areas, including manufactured homes, RVs, and tiny homes.
Translation: "No zoning" does not mean "no rules." Floodplain, OSSF, driveway/culvert, subdivision restrictions, private deed restrictions, utility easements, and lender/insurance rules can still block plans.

Use Cases to Test

Primary residenceMost straightforward if inspections and financing clear.
Long-term rentalModel rent conservatively; manufactured-home tenant pool and maintenance are different from stick-built homes.
Workshop / storageCheck deed restrictions, access, power, impervious cover, and neighbors.
RV / tiny home / extra dwellingRequires development permit analysis and septic capacity review.
Animals / agricultureVerify subdivision restrictions, fencing, water, and nuisance rules.

Physical Site Questions

Access and frontage
Confirm driveway location, culvert condition, emergency access, delivery/trailer turnarounds, and whether any road work or easement is needed.
Drainage and buildable area
Walk the property after rain if possible. Map low spots, channels, ponding, erosion, and where future structures would sit. Do not stop at FEMA Zone X if local drainage shows a problem.
Utilities
PDF research points to Aqua Water Supply, Bluebonnet Electric, propane, and cable/broadband. Verify actual provider, account transfer, service capacity, buried lines, and outage history before assuming future use.
