Who Can Claim Homestead?

RequiredThe buyer owns and actually occupies the property as a principal residence.
RequiredThe buyer does not claim a residence homestead on another property.
NoA pure rental or investment-property plan does not qualify for the residence homestead exemption.
Manufactured homeManufactured-home status alone does not disqualify the land and home. Tax Code 11.432 permits homestead treatment when ownership and residence requirements are met, even if the home is listed separately.
Closing-year nuance: immediate mid-year qualification is available only when the preceding owner did not receive the same exemption. Because this parcel already shows seller HS/HSLOC, assume the buyer must establish their own qualification for the following January 1 unless Bastrop CAD confirms otherwise.

How to Apply

1. Establish principal residence
Occupy the property and update the Texas driver license or state ID so the address matches the application.
2. Complete Form 50-114
File the free Bastrop residence-homestead application; do not pay a filing service.
3. Attach ownership evidence
Include matching ID, closing/ownership evidence, and manufactured-home Statement of Ownership or other documentation requested on the form.
4. Submit a legible PDF
Email to exemptions@bastropcad.org, mail to P.O. Box 578, Bastrop, TX 78602, or use the drop box at 212 Jackson Street. Bastrop CAD says photo images are not accepted.
5. Confirm the effective year
Ask Bastrop CAD how the seller's existing exemption affects the closing year and verify the buyer's exemption and cap start date in the property record.
