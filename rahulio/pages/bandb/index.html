Welcome Awakening

Something within you is stirring

A longing for deeper breath, for freedom from the knots that bind,
for the sweet release of remembrance:
You are scintillating poetry; woven of waters, earth, and sky.

This is a sanctuary that honors the journey of being human, in all its beautiful complexity — the ache and the ecstasy, the mundane and the mystical. Here, the corporeal body is regarded as a sacred vessel of Spirit, deserving of reverence and care.

Whether you arrive in need of relief, revelation, or simply a place to be held, you are met exactly where you are. Through the ancient arts of touch, divination, plant medicine, and deep listening, we tend to the places within you that have been neglected, forgotten, or exiled.

This is not about fixing what is broken. It is about remembering what is whole.

Those Whom I Serve

My practice is a refuge for the sensitive, the seeking, and the sovereign. If you see yourself reflected below, you are welcome here.

Artists, Performers, Muses & Creatives

Those whose bodies are instruments of expression, often carrying the weight of emotional labor, performance anxiety, and creative depletion.

Spiritual Seekers, Mystics & Practitioners

Those walking devotional paths who desire somatic support, energetic attunement, and grounded spiritual companionship.

Caregivers, Healthcare & Wellness Workers

Those who hold space for others and rarely receive the depth of care they give. Healers who need healing.

Survivors of Trauma, Negligence & Abuse

Those seeking a safe, consent-centered, trauma-informed environment to begin reclaiming the body as home.

Individuals in High-Pressure Careers

Executives, attorneys, physicians, and leaders carrying the somatic burden of chronic stress and decision fatigue.

Chronic Pain & Complex Medical Conditions

Those navigating autoimmune conditions, fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue, and other persistent challenges in the body.

Grief Carriers & Earth Mourners

Those tending to loss — of loved ones, of homelands, of ecosystems — who need space to grieve fully and be witnessed.

Reclaiming Embodied Sensual Expression

Those healing from shame, disconnection, or trauma around the body's innate capacity for pleasure and aliveness.

LGBTQIA+ & Gender Identity Exploration

Those exploring gender, sexuality, and embodiment who desire affirming, nonjudgmental somatic care.

Activists, Organizers & Changemakers

Those on the front lines of social and ecological justice who carry collective grief and burnout in their bodies.

Invisible Illnesses & Disabilities

Those whose conditions are unseen or dismissed, who seek a practitioner willing to listen to the body's quiet truths.

Academics, Philosophers & Deep Thinkers

Those who live richly in the mind and long for integration with the body, sensation, and the felt experience of being alive.

Recovery, Sobriety & New Beginnings

Those navigating the tender terrain of early recovery and rebuilding a loving relationship with the body.

Neurodivergent Individuals

Those with ADHD, autism, sensory processing differences, and other neurodivergences who benefit from adaptive, attuned bodywork.

Multicultural Diaspora, Immigrants & Refugees

Those carrying the weight of displacement, cultural grief, and the complex longing for belonging and ancestral reconnection.

Healing from Medical or Surgical Trauma

Those whose bodies carry the imprint of institutional or medical interventions and seek gentle, restorative somatic care.

Ceremony & Interdimensional Integration

Those who have undergone plant medicine ceremonies or deep spiritual experiences and need support integrating the journey.

Rites of Passage & Life Transitions

Those navigating initiations, births, deaths, divorces, career shifts, and the great thresholds of personal evolution.

An Ecosystem of Holistic Health and Gaian Medicine

Oracular Poet · Diviner · Touch Therapist · Medicine Woman · Spiritual Midwife

For over fourteen years, I have devoted my life to the sacred intersection of body, spirit, and earth. As a nationally licensed massage therapist, initiated diviner, and ceremonial facilitator, my practice draws from the deep wells of ancestral wisdom, somatics, bioenergetics, and the living intelligence of the plant world.

My lineage is a braided river — Mesoamerican, Caribbean, Celtic, and Roma — and it is through this confluence of traditions that I approach the work of healing. I am a Priestess of the Dagara Crocodile Clan, trained in the arts of stick divination, and a student of Generative Trance Hypnosis under the mentorship of Stephen Gilligan.

For over fifteen years I have studied folk and clinical ethnopharmacology, tending to the relationship between human bodies and the botanical world. Plants are my first teachers, and the garden is my first temple.

The corporeal body is a sacred vessel of Spirit — this is the root of everything I offer. Whether through the alchemy of touch, the oracle of divination, or the counsel of plant allies, I am here to support your remembering.
