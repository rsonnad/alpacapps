There is a real capacity constraint to investigate, but profitable GPU arbitrage is not yet demonstrated. On 22 October 2026, Scaleway’s official inventory API reported shortages for the H100 and L40S configurations checked in Warsaw. That is a useful sales signal. It is not proof that Poland or Europe has run out of GPUs, that the constraint will last through a hardware loan, or that buyers will pay a new provider enough to cover its costs. The best starting position for a commercially strong founder is to assemble a matched supply-and-customer deal, then decide whether to broker, aggregate, operate or own the capacity.

2. The apparent spread can disappear before electricity matters

Atman’s two-L40S 36-month offer divides into €1.33 per available GPU-hour: €1,942 ÷ (2 × 730). At 50% paid occupancy it becomes €2.66 per sold GPU-hour, before your support, acquisition and other overhead. At the published Scaleway L40S hourly rate, two continuously sold GPUs would generate about €2,146 monthly—only €204 above that Atman rent, before overhead and without proving equivalent contracts. The 12-month Atman tariff alone is €2.59 per available GPU-hour. Availability might justify a premium; the arithmetic does not establish one.

Billed utilization is not GPU activity. A customer can pay to reserve a mostly idle server; unsold idle capacity is your risk. Compare exact GPU edition, memory, topology, accepted workload throughput, location, SLA and noncancelable term. Never sell consumer RTX performance as H100 equivalence. A cheap bare chassis is not an installed GPU system: published configurator starting prices may exclude the cards. Obtain the complete bill of materials, installation, warranty and replacement lead time. Example configurator.

The calculator below uses generic illustrative inputs, not a named GPU or market quotation: four GPUs, PLN240,000 installed capital, zero salvage, 36-month life, 70% billed utilization and PLN6 per sold GPU-hour. Revenue is PLN12,264/month. Average IT draw of 2.4kW × 730h × 1.5 overhead × PLN0.90 costs PLN2,365.20; other cash cost is PLN12,000. Operating cash flow is therefore −PLN2,101.20. Adding PLN6,666.67 monthly hardware recovery gives full cost PLN21,031.87, an economic result of −PLN8,767.87, and a floor of PLN10.29 per sold GPU-hour. Break-even at PLN6 requires roughly 120% billed utilization: infeasible. This does not price an H100; it demonstrates how unsupported assumptions can defeat a seemingly attractive spread.

Northwest Poland’s power advantage still needs a written delivered quote. Pomerania Cloud’s subsidized rack tariff bills electricity separately with a 1.8 infrastructure multiplier; confirm eligibility, GPU density and available power. Do not apply a second cooling multiplier to an all-in offer. At the generic model’s fixed load, a PLN0.20/kWh discount saves only PLN525.60/month. Pomerania Cloud.

5. Invest only after the deal survives downside

Require two independent written supply offers, a paid benchmark accepted by the buyer, one creditworthy anchor minimum-spend contract, explicit resale rights, signed supply/finance conditions and a named operator. Match supplier noncancelable exposure to buyer commitments. If matching is impossible, quantify maximum loss, cap it to affordable risk capital and prefer brokerage.

Model hardware recovery or lease, power, colocation, connectivity, licenses, operations, spares, insurance, financing/FX, sales/admin and SLA credits. Do not double-count cash capex, loan principal and depreciation as the same cost. Test a 20% selling-price reduction, 25% electricity increase, 60-day payment delay, realistic outage/credits and no second customer. These are proposed stress scenarios. Use zero hardware salvage conservatively; set a written return hurdle and cash reserve with the financier. If the downside creates an unfunded gap, shrink or reject the deal.

The opportunity is to make difficult capacity procurement dependable. Profitable ownership can follow, but the first asset should be a matched, technically accepted contract. No interviews, private quotes, deposits, reservations or customer commitments have been obtained in this research; the campaign above is the route to obtaining that proof.
