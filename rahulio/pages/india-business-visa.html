Visa & Business Guide

Getting an Indian Visa by Starting a Business

A step-by-step walkthrough for EU citizens who want to incorporate a company in India and obtain a long-term business visa.

Last updated: Feb 2026 | For EU/EEA passport holders

🧭 Find Your Best Path

Answer a few questions and we'll highlight the steps most relevant to your situation.

ℹ️
Why the business route? India does not offer a general long-term residence visa for foreigners. But if you incorporate a company in India, you can obtain a Business Visa (up to 5 years, multiple entry) or an Employment Visa tied to your own company. This guide covers the business incorporation path — the most common route for EU entrepreneurs.

Steps

  1. Understand the visa landscape
  2. Choose your business structure
  3. Get a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)
  4. Reserve your company name
  5. Incorporate via SPICe+ on MCA
  6. Open an Indian bank account
  7. Infuse capital & file RBI compliance
  8. Apply for DPIIT Startup Recognition (optional)
  9. Apply for your Business Visa
  10. Register with FRRO on arrival
  11. Maintain compliance & renew
1

Understand the Visa Landscape

India offers several visa categories. For an EU citizen starting a business, the two most relevant are the Business Visa and the Business e-Visa. Here's how they compare:

Feature Business e-Visa Business Visa (sticker)
Validity 1 year 1 – 5 years
Max stay per visit 180 days 180 days (register at FRRO if > 180)
Entries Multiple Multiple
Apply where Online (indianvisaonline.gov.in) Indian Embassy/Consulate or BLS/VFS center
Processing 3 – 5 business days 5 – 15 business days
Best for Quick entry, exploratory trips Long-term presence, company directors
💡
Recommended approach Start with a Business e-Visa (fast to get, no embassy visit). Use it to enter India, incorporate your company, open a bank account. Then apply for a full 5-year Business Visa from your home country using the Certificate of Incorporation as supporting documentation.
2

Choose Your Business Structure

As a foreign national, you can register the following types of entities in India:

Structure Min Directors Indian Resident Director? FDI Allowed?
Private Limited Company Recommended 2 Yes, at least 1 Yes (100% in most sectors)
Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) 2 partners Yes, at least 1 Yes (with conditions)
Branch Office N/A Local authorized rep RBI approval required
Wholly Owned Subsidiary 2 Yes, at least 1 Yes
⚠️
Indian Resident Director requirement At least one director must have resided in India for 182+ days in the previous calendar year. If you don't have an Indian co-founder, you'll need to appoint a nominee resident director. Professional firms (CS/CA firms) offer this service for INR 15,000 – 50,000/year.

For most EU entrepreneurs, a Private Limited Company is the best choice — it’s the simplest to set up, qualifies for DPIIT Startup India benefits, and gives you the Certificate of Incorporation needed for a long-term Business Visa.

3

Get a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)

Every proposed director needs a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate to sign documents electronically on India's MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) portal. This is the first mandatory step.

📋 What you need

  • Valid passport (scanned color copy)
  • Proof of address in your EU country (utility bill, bank statement — less than 3 months old)
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Email address and phone number
  • All documents notarized & apostilled (Hague Apostille from your country) OR notarized by the Indian Embassy in your country

🏢 Where to apply

  • eMudhra (emudhra.com) — largest DSC provider, supports foreign applicants
  • Sify / NSDL / CDSL — other authorized certifying authorities
  • You can also go through a Chartered Accountant (CA) or Company Secretary (CS) firm who will handle this for you

Cost: INR 1,500 – 3,000 (~EUR 16 – 32). Valid for 2 years.

ℹ️
Apostille vs. Embassy notarization India is a member of the Hague Apostille Convention. EU countries issue apostilles through designated authorities (usually the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or a court). An apostilled document is accepted directly — no separate Indian Embassy attestation needed.
4

Reserve Your Company Name

Name reservation is done through SPICe+ Part A on the MCA portal. This can be done before you arrive in India.

🔤 Rules for naming

  • Cannot be identical or too similar to existing companies (search at mca.gov.in)
  • Cannot contain words like "Government", "Reserve Bank", "United Nations"
  • Must end with "Private Limited" for a Pvt Ltd company
  • You can submit up to 2 name choices
Go to mca.gov.in → MCA Services → Company → SPICe+ Part A

Log in (or create account) on the MCA portal.

Fill in your preferred company names

Provide 2 options. Include the proposed activity/industry description.

Submit and pay the fee

INR 1,000 (~EUR 11). Payment via net banking, credit card, or debit card.

Wait for approval

Typically 1 – 3 business days. Name reservation is valid for 20 days.

5

Incorporate via SPICe+ on MCA

SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus) is a single form that handles company incorporation, PAN, TAN, GST, EPFO, ESIC, and bank account opening — all at once.

📄 Documents required from foreign directors

  • Passport (scanned, color, apostilled)
  • Proof of address in home country (apostilled)
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) — from Step 3
  • Director Identification Number (DIN) — applied within SPICe+ itself
  • Subscriber sheet of MoA & AoA (must be signed & witnessed separately for foreign nationals)
  • Declaration in Form INC-9 (signed with DSC)
  • Consent to act as Director (DIR-2)
  • Indian registered office address proof (rental agreement + utility bill + NOC from landlord)

⚙️ SPICe+ Part B walkthrough

  1. Company details — Type (Private Limited), category (Company limited by shares), sub-category (Non-government), authorized capital
  2. Directors — Enter details for all directors. DIN is generated automatically for new directors.
  3. Registered office — Indian address with proof documents
  4. Stamp duty — E-stamp duty based on authorized capital and state
  5. AGILE-PRO-S — Integrated form for GSTIN, EPFO, ESIC, and bank account
  6. eMoA & eAoA — Electronic Memorandum & Articles of Association (auto-generated templates)
  7. Upload attachments — All apostilled documents, photos, declarations
  8. Submit & pay — Filing fee depends on authorized capital (INR 500 – 15,000+)
💡
Use a professional While you can technically do this yourself, hiring a Company Secretary (CS) or Chartered Accountant (CA) is strongly recommended. They handle the MCA portal quirks, document formatting, and any Registrar of Companies (RoC) queries. Budget INR 15,000 – 40,000 (~EUR 160 – 430) for full-service incorporation.
Certificate of Incorporation (CIN) issued

Usually within 3 – 7 business days if documents are clean. Comes with PAN and TAN.

GSTIN issued

GST registration number generated through integrated AGILE-PRO-S.

Your company now legally exists

Keep the CIN safe — you'll need it for the visa application.

6

Open an Indian Bank Account

Your newly incorporated company needs an Indian current (business) account. Some banks allow opening during SPICe+ (AGILE-PRO-S), but in practice you'll often need to visit a branch.

🏦 Recommended banks for foreign-owned companies

  • HDFC Bank — Widely used, good online banking, foreign-director-friendly
  • ICICI Bank — Good startup banking programs
  • Kotak Mahindra Bank — Competitive, digital-forward
  • SBI — State Bank, required for some government dealings

📋 Documents needed

  • Certificate of Incorporation
  • MoA and AoA
  • PAN card of the company
  • Board resolution authorizing account opening
  • KYC of all directors (passport, address proof, photos)
  • Proof of registered office (rental agreement + utility bill)
⚠️
Plan to be physically in India Most banks require at least one in-person visit by a director for KYC verification. Some private banks (HDFC, ICICI) offer video KYC for certain cases, but expect to visit a branch.
7

Infuse Capital & File RBI Compliance

As a foreign shareholder, your capital investment must follow India's Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) rules administered by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

💰 Capital infusion process

  1. Transfer funds from your EU bank account to the Indian company's bank account via SWIFT wire transfer
  2. The bank will credit the amount into the company's current account
  3. Allot shares to yourself within 60 days of receiving the funds
  4. File FC-GPR (Foreign Currency – Gross Provisional Return) with RBI within 30 days of share allotment
  5. File Form FC-GPR on the RBI's FIRMS portal (firms.rbi.org.in)
🇮🇳
FDI is allowed up to 100% in most sectors Under the automatic route, no government approval is needed for most business activities. Restricted sectors (defense, telecom, media, multi-brand retail) require government approval. Consult the DPIIT's Consolidated FDI Policy for the latest list.
🚫
Do NOT skip the FC-GPR filing Late or missing FC-GPR filings can result in penalties up to 3x the amount involved. This is the most commonly missed compliance step for foreign founders. Your CA/CS should handle this.
8

Apply for DPIIT Startup Recognition (Optional)

If your company qualifies as a "startup" under India's Startup India initiative, DPIIT recognition unlocks significant tax benefits and credibility.

✅ Eligibility criteria

  • Incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm
  • Less than 10 years since incorporation
  • Annual turnover under INR 100 crore (~EUR 11M)
  • Working on innovation, development, or improvement of products/processes/services
  • Not formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business

🎁 Benefits of DPIIT recognition

  • Tax holiday — 3 out of first 10 years of incorporation (Section 80-IAC)
  • Angel tax exemption — No tax on share premium (Section 56(2)(viib))
  • Patent/trademark fee rebates — 80% discount on patent filing
  • Self-certification for labor and environment laws
  • Government procurement eligibility without prior experience
  • Fund of Funds — Access to SIDBI's INR 10,000 crore fund
Register at startupindia.gov.in

Create a profile on the Startup India portal with your company details.

Fill the DPIIT recognition form

Describe your business, innovation, and upload Certificate of Incorporation.

Submit and wait

Approval typically takes 1 – 2 weeks. You'll receive a Recognition Certificate.

9

Apply for Your Business Visa

With your Certificate of Incorporation in hand, you can now apply for a full Business Visa (up to 5 years, multiple entry) from the Indian Embassy or Consulate in your EU country.

📋 Documents required

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity, 2+ blank pages)
  • Completed visa application form (online at indianvisaonline.gov.in)
  • 2 passport-size photos (51mm x 51mm, white background)
  • Certificate of Incorporation of your Indian company
  • Invitation letter from your Indian company (on company letterhead)
  • Business plan or description of activities
  • Proof of financial standing (bank statements, last 6 months)
  • Cover letter explaining your business purpose in India
  • Company PAN card copy
  • Board resolution appointing you as director/representative

🏛️ Where to apply (EU countries)

Visa applications are processed through BLS International or VFS Global (depending on your country) on behalf of the Indian Embassy. Check your country's specific center:

  • Germany — BLS International (Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt)
  • France — VFS Global (Paris)
  • Netherlands — VFS Global (The Hague)
  • Spain — BLS International (Madrid)
  • Italy — BLS International (Rome, Milan)
  • Other EU countries — check indianembassy.org or blsindia-visa.com

💶 Fees (approximate for EU citizens)

Duration Fee
Up to 6 months EUR 82
Up to 1 year EUR 82 – 132
Up to 5 years EUR 132 – 200

Fees vary by nationality. Some EU countries have bilateral agreements with India that affect pricing. BLS/VFS service charges are additional.

💡
Apply for the maximum duration Always request the 5-year multiple-entry visa. With a registered Indian company, approval for the longer duration is standard. Having the DPIIT Startup Recognition certificate further strengthens your application.
10

Register with FRRO on Arrival

If you plan to stay in India for more than 180 consecutive days, you must register with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) within 14 days of arrival.

🖥️ Online registration process

  1. Go to indianfrro.gov.in
  2. Create an account and fill in your details
  3. Upload passport, visa, photos, proof of address in India
  4. Book an appointment at your local FRRO
  5. Visit the FRRO office with originals for verification
  6. Receive your Registration Certificate (usually same day or within 24 hours)

Registration fee: ~INR 100 (~EUR 1). Locations: Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Amritsar, and others.

⚠️
FRRO registration is mandatory Failure to register is a punishable offense under the Registration of Foreigners Act. Even if your visa is valid for 5 years, each continuous stay exceeding 180 days requires FRRO registration.
11

Maintain Compliance & Renew

Once your company is running and you're in India on a Business Visa, there are ongoing compliance requirements to keep everything valid.

📅 Annual company compliance

  • Annual Return (AOC-4 + MGT-7) — filed with RoC within 30/60 days of AGM
  • Income Tax Return — due September 30 each year (if audit required)
  • GST Returns — monthly (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) or quarterly (QRMP scheme)
  • Board meetings — minimum 4 per year, at least 1 every 120 days
  • Annual General Meeting (AGM) — within 6 months of financial year end (March 31)
  • Statutory audit — mandatory for all companies (appoint a CA as auditor)

📊 Business visa turnover requirement

For visa extension, the Indian government may require:

  • Gross sales or turnover of at least INR 10 million (~EUR 107,000) per annum
  • This must be achieved within 2 years of setting up the business or from the date of initial visa grant
  • In practice, this is checked at renewal — not enforced for the first visa term

🔄 Visa renewal

  • Apply at FRRO (if in India) or at the Indian Embassy (if outside India)
  • Provide updated company financials, tax returns, and bank statements
  • Renewal for another 5 years is standard if the company is active
🚫
Do NOT overstay your visa Overstaying even by a day can result in fines, deportation, and future visa bans. Set calendar reminders for your visa expiry and FRRO registration renewal dates.

Submit SPICe+ Part B, receive Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, TAN, GSTIN.
