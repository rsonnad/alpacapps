MEXC vs Hyperliquid

Both exchanges offer perpetual futures on US equities. MEXC leads on volume (~$12.6B daily) and fees (0% maker / 0.02% taker). Hyperliquid counters with full decentralization, self-custody, no KYC, and permissionless market creation via HIP-3.

MEXC CEX
  • Launched mid-2025, USDT-margined
  • 13 tickers: COIN, HOOD, NVDA, AAPL, AMZN, GOOGL, META, TSLA, MCD, IREN, LMT, OXY, ACHR
  • Prices reference Ondo tokenized stocks
  • US market hours only
  • Up to 100x leverage
  • Funding fees currently waived
Hyperliquid DEX
  • Launched Oct 2025 via HIP-3 protocol
  • 12+ tickers: NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, MSFT, PLTR, META, AMZN, GOOGL, AMD, COIN, NFLX, SpaceX
  • XYZ100 tracks Nasdaq-100 ($80M+ daily vol)
  • HyperStone oracle (3s updates, 4-of-6 quorum)
  • Near 24/7 trading
  • Permissionless deployment (500K HYPE stake)
