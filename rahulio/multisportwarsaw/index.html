Does the MultiSport card pay off?

MultiSport (by Benefit Systems) is a single card that gets you into thousands of gyms, pools, climbing walls, studios and courts across Poland — one entry per venue per day. Here’s the quick economics for a stay in Warsaw.

📋 How entry works

  • Personalised card. Photo + ID checked at the desk — it’s not transferable.
  • One entry per venue per day. Re‑entry to the same place needs another day.
  • Classic vs Unlimited. Classic = one facility a day; Unlimited lets you hit several.
  • Book classes in the app. Yoga, Pilates and pool lanes often need a reservation.
  • Bring a padlock & flip‑flops. Standard for Polish gyms and pools.

⚠️ Surcharge gotchas

  • Climbing. Bouldering is often free before 3pm on weekdays; after 3pm & weekends add ~20 zł. Shoe/harness rental is extra.
  • Pools. Entry is usually time‑limited (~45–90 min); some aqua parks restrict zones/saunas.
  • Racket sports. Tennis / squash / badminton are usually only partly covered — you reserve and may top up court time.
  • Boutique studios. Many yoga/Pilates studios cap you at a set number of entries per month.
  • Always confirm in the app. Partner terms change per venue.

At ~45 zł a visit, Unlimited (280 zł) breaks even at ~6–7 visits/month, and the 8× plan at ~3. Go three times a week and the card is a rounding error — plus you can sample a different gym, pool or wall every day on Unlimited.

Visitor tip: MultiSport is an employer/association benefit, not sold to tourists at the door. Get it via a Polish employer, a coworking/association membership, or look at equivalent cards (Medicover Sport, FitProfit, OK System) that share much of the same venue list.
