About event hosting

A community venue with a lot of personality

The Austin Alpaca Playhouse is a community venue in Cedar Creek, Texas, 13 minutes east of Austin Airport. Guests can meet the alpacas, warm up in the wood-fired sauna, take a cold plunge, and gather inside or out.

Note: no cooking meat inside the house or storing meat in the kitchen refrigerator. Meat can be cooked on the back patio grill area (grill, oven, air fryer) and stored in other refrigerators.

  • Retreats & team gatherings

Before you applyVenue rules & requirements ▼

👥Staffing

As the host, you provide:

  • Setup staff: arrive 90 minutes before the event starts
  • Cleanup crew: cleaning done by 1:01pm the next day
  • Parking coordinator: keeps cars off neighbors' property and off the wet grass all event long. You are responsible for any parking problems that happen.

🎉Guest limits

Your approved guest count goes in the agreement. Each extra person beyond it is $15. You agree to report actual attendance honestly.

🔒Address privacy

Do not post our address in marketing, texts, emails or social media. Send guests to alpacaplayhouse.com/visiting for directions, parking, alpaca safety, food and venue rules.

$100 if the address is posted publicly.

🎵Noise & quiet hours

Keep outdoor noise and music low after 9:30pm. Keep doors closed when loud music plays inside. No loud PA speakers outdoors after 9:30pm.

$100 if neighbors complain about noise.

🚗Parking

  • Use the gravel lot inside the gate; park near other cars, facing the street or container
  • Never park on grass (sprinklers, mud)
  • Keep the circle, garage, sauna area and delivery access clear
  • Overflow: across the street or on the frontage road (5-minute walk)

$150 per neighbor complaint about parking.

🦙Alpaca & gate safety

Always keep the back fence gates closed so the alpacas can't escape. Please call them "alpacas," not "llamas." Keep dogs and pets out of the back yard; pets may only be tied in the front yard.

🏠House rules

  • No alcohol at the house or at events
  • No cooking meat inside or storing it in the kitchen fridge; use the back patio grill area
  • No animals in the house or back yard; owners must keep their dogs in sight, or tie them in the front yard only ($100 for violations)
  • No RVs on the property; park on the frontage road ($100)
  • Wash and replace any linens and towels you use
  • Put furniture back where it was (photograph it before moving)
  • Propane use is deducted from the deposit

🧹Cleaning

You clean up after your event. Once approved, you get a checklist made for your event (sauna use means washing, drying and folding every towel) and a link to upload before and after photos of each area.

A cleaning inspector checks the house. If it isn't clean, we finish it and take the cost from your deposit (see pricing).
