## Platform-Specific Notes

### 1-4. Facebook Groups
- Use the Thai copy above
- Search Facebook for these groups and request to join if not already a member:
  - "Jobs in Bangkok and Thailand (non teaching)" (~47K members)
  - "Bangkok, Thailand, Jobs, Recruiters and Head Hunters" (~35K members)
  - "Farang Jobs in Chiang Mai Thailand" (~800 members)
  - Search: "หางานนวดต่างประเทศ", "งานสปาต่างประเทศ"

### 5. Spa Company Bangkok (spa-company.com)
- **Dedicated Thai therapist recruiting portal** — highest signal platform
- Create an employer account
- Post the English copy; therapists browse and apply directly
- Paid service but has a database of active job-seeking therapists

### 6. JobThai (jobthai.com)
- Thailand's #1 job board
- Post in Thai using the Thai copy
- Category: สุขภาพ/ฟิตเนส/สปา (Health/Fitness/Spa)
- Free basic posting available

### 7. Jobsdb Thailand (th.jobsdb.com)
- Major regional board (SEEK-owned)
- Post in both Thai and English
- Category: Healthcare / Beauty & Fitness

### 8. SpaStaff.com
- Global spa recruitment — free job postings
- Use the English copy
- Has CV search facility

### 9. JobBKK (jobbkk.com)
- Another major Thai board
- Post in Thai

### 10. Expat.com Thailand
- Targets internationally-minded Thais and expats
- Use English copy

### 11. Thai Massage Schools (Direct Outreach)
Contact these schools about posting on their job boards or referring graduates:
- **Wat Pho Thai Traditional Massage School** (Bangkok) — the most famous
- **TMC (Thai Massage School of Chiang Mai)** — large school, many graduates
- **ITM (International Training Massage School)** — Chiang Mai, international focus
- **Baan Hom Samunphrai** — Chiang Mai, smaller but high quality

### 12. LINE Groups
- Thailand's dominant messaging app
- Massage/spa community shares jobs through LINE groups
- These are invite-only — ask any Thai contacts to share in their networks
- This is often the fastest way to reach active therapists
