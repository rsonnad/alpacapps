Mistiq Massage is the Chiang Mai branch of the Mistique Journey, a breath and body experience that began in Austin, Texas. It is a 2-hour hands-on guided experience designed to enable deep physical and emotional transformation in the body.

The Journey is a structured set of activities which encourage the participant to focus their breath on opening all the muscles that hold tension and block energetic flow. This process allows participants to progressively deepen their mental clarity and emotional regulation, while enabling both deep physical relaxation and invigoration.

For hands-on elements of the Journey, the practitioner guides the breath of the participant through touch, while the participant guides the practitioner through hand communications. This system promotes the delivery of nothing more or less than what is best aligned with the needs of the participant at any given time.

The Journey Process

A carefully structured 2-hour process designed to guide you through transformation

"Grounding" — Phase 1: 15 minutes

Begin your journey with centering practices that help you arrive fully in your body. Through guided meditation and gentle awareness exercises, you'll establish a foundation of presence.

This phase sets the tone for your entire experience, helping you leave behind daily stress and connect with your breath and body.

"Somatics" — Phase 2: ~75 minutes

The core transformational work happens during this extended phase. Through a combination of assisted yoga, Thai massage, breathwork, the ice blanket, hot herbal compress and oil massage, you'll experience a deep release of physical and emotional patterns.

Your practitioner will guide you through this process with attentive care, responding to your body's unique needs.

"Wind Down" — Phase 3: 15 minutes

As your journey concludes, this phase helps you integrate the experience. Rest and subtle energy work allow your nervous system to recalibrate.

This transition helps your body recognize and retain the benefits of your profound work.

"Integration" — Phase 4: 15 minutes

The final minutes are dedicated to integration: quiet reflection, a cup of tea, or gentle conversation, giving you space to process insights before stepping back into everyday life.
