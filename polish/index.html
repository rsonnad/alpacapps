Pronunciation Guide

Polish Sounds — Learn by Word

cz
= ch (like "church")
Cześć — hello czas — time człowiek — person CHWOH-vyek
sz
= sh (like "shoe")
Proszę — please szkoła — school SHKOH-wah masz — you have
ż / rz
= zh (like "measure")
wieczór — evening może — maybe MOH-zheh rzeka — river ZHEH-kah
dz
= dz (like end of "odds")
widzenia — seeing (goodbye) dzień — day jen dziecko — child JETS-koh
ś / si
= soft sh (like "sheep" but softer)
jeśtem — I am prośba — request PROSH-bah siedem — seven SHEH-dem
ć / ci
= soft ch (like "cheap" but softer)
Cześć — hi pięć — five pyench ciepło — warm CHEP-woh
j
= y (like "yes" — never English "j")
jadę — I'm driving jutro — tomorrow YOO-troh jest — is
w
= v (like "vine" — never English "w")
widzenia — goodbye woda — water VOH-dah wieczór — evening
ł
= w (like "water" — this IS the "w" sound)
skłepu — store szkoła — school SHKOH-wah ciepło — warm CHEP-woh
ę
= nasal en (say "eh" through your nose)
jadę — I'm driving idę — I'm walking będę — I will be BEN-deh
ą
= nasal on (say "oh" through your nose)
są — they are sohn mają — they have MY-ohn kąpiel — bath KOHM-pyel

Stress is almost always on the second-to-last syllable. Every letter is pronounced — no silent letters.
