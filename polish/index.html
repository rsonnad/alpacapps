Alpaca Playhouse Polish — Lesson 1
Pronunciation Guide

Polish Sounds — Learn by Word

cz
= ch (like "church")
Cześć — hello czas — time człowiek — person CHWOH-vyek
sz
= sh (like "shoe")
Proszę — please szkoła — school SHKOH-wah masz — you have
ż / rz
= zh (like "measure")
wieczór — evening może — maybe MOH-zheh rzeka — river ZHEH-kah
dz
= dz (like end of "odds")
dzwon — bell dzvon dzban — jug dzban widzę — I see VEE-dzeh
ś / si
= soft sh (like "sheep" but softer)
jestem — I am prośba — request PROSH-bah siedem — seven SHEH-dem
ć / ci
= soft ch (like "cheap" but softer)
Cześć — hi pięć — five pyench ciepło — warm CHEP-woh
j
= y (like "yes" — never English "j")
jadę — I'm driving jutro — tomorrow YOO-troh jest — is
w
= v (like "vine" — Polish w = English v)
widzenia — goodbye woda — water VOH-dah wieczór — evening
ł
= w (like "water" — Polish ł = English w)
dała — she gave DAH-wah szkoła — school SHKOH-wah ciepło — warm CHEP-woh
ę
= nasal en (say "eh" through your nose)
jadę — I'm driving idę — I'm walking będę — I will be BEN-deh
ą
= nasal on (say "oh" through your nose)
są — they are sohn mają — they have MY-ohn kąpiel — bath KOHM-pyel
ck
= ts-k (two sounds: "ts" then "k")
tacka — tray TATS-kah klocek — block KLOH-tsek placek — cake PLAH-tsek
ch
= h (same as Polish h — breathy)
chleb — bread hlep chcę — I want h-tseh chodź — come! hohdj
szcz
= shch (like "fresh cheese" blended)
szczęście — happiness SHCHEN-shcheh deszcz — rain deshch jeszcze — still/yet YESH-cheh
dź / dzi
= soft j (like "jeans" but softer)
dzień — day jeñ dziecko — child JETS-koh dziękuję — thank you jen-KOO-yeh
dż
= hard j (like "jam")
dżem — jam jem dżungla — jungle JOONG-lah dżinsy — jeans JIN-sy
ź / zi
= soft zh (like "vision" but softer)
źle — badly zhleh zima — winter ZHEE-mah zielony — green zheh-LOH-ny
ń / ni
= ny (like "canyon")
koń — horse koñ słoń — elephant swoñ nie — no nyeh
c
= ts (like "cats" — never English "k")
co — what tsoh noc — night nots cena — price TSEH-nah

Swap alert: Polish w = English "v" and Polish ł = English "w". So woda (water) is "VOH-dah" and szkoła (school) is "SHKOH-wah".

Stress is almost always on the second-to-last syllable. Every letter is pronounced — no silent letters.
