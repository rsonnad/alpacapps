Pronunciation Guide

Polish Sounds — Learn by Word

cz ch — Cześć (hello) · like "church"
sz sh — Proszę (please) · like "shoe"
ż / rz zh — Dobry wieczór (good evening) · like "measure"
dz dz — Do widzenia (goodbye) · like end of "odds"
ś / si soft sh — jeśtem (I am) · like "sheep" but softer
ć / ci soft ch — Cześć (hi) · like "cheap" but softer
j y — Jadę (I'm driving) · like "yes" — never English "j"
w v — Do widzenia (goodbye) · like "vine" — never English "w"
ł w — skłepu (store) · like "water" — this IS the "w" sound
ę nasal en — Jadę (I'm driving) · say "eh" through your nose
ą nasal on — samochód (ó = oo) · say "oh" through your nose

Stress is almost always on the second-to-last syllable. Every letter is pronounced — no silent letters.
