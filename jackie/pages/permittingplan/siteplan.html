Two Public Road Frontages

Property fronts on Still Forest Dr (CR 329) to the south AND Leisure Lane (CR 331) to the west. Both are 60' ROW county roads. Per Bastrop County Lodging/RV Regs Section IV.8(a), lodging units require 25' setbacks from public ROW.

Setback Violations (Lodging Units Only)

Big Trailer (24' deficit) and Small Trailer (~8') are definite violations as RV/lodging units. Containers are violations only if used as lodging — storage-only containers may not be regulated under Section IV.8. Bathroom building needs a permit regardless.

  • Containers: If used strictly for storage (not dwelling/lodging), containers may not be subject to Section IV.8 setbacks. If adapted for lodging use, the 25' ROW setback applies.
  • Bathroom Building: Built AFTER the survey — under construction WITHOUT a permit. Classified as a service building (Section IV.11), not a Lodging Development Unit. Still requires a Bastrop County Development Permit (Section I.5.a — required for all permanent structures).
  • ESD: Property is in BCESD #3. ESD letter required for IDP.
  • Flood Zone: Zone X (unshaded) — NOT in 100-year floodplain.
  • Houston Toad: NOT in habitat area — no LPHCP Declination needed.
  • Definite Violations: Big Trailer (24' deficit from ROW) and Small Trailer (~8' deficit from property line) are RV/lodging units that clearly violate setbacks. Must relocate or remove.
