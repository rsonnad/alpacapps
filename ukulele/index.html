💡 Tips

Finger pressure: Press strings with your fingertips, right behind the fret (not on it). You need less pressure than you think.
Strumming: Use the pad of your index finger or thumb. Strum from the G string down to the A string. Keep your wrist loose.
Chord changes: Practice switching between just two chords at first (C → Am is easiest). Speed comes with muscle memory.
Buzzing strings? Check that your fingers are pressing firmly and aren't accidentally touching neighboring strings.
