## 1. Finding

**Still Forest Dr is a Bastrop County-maintained road (not a TxDOT state highway).** Confidence: **high** (inferential — based on naming convention + county permit page evidence, not yet row-confirmed against TxDOT inventory).

No TxDOT state highway carries the name "Still Forest Dr." TxDOT on-system roads use IH/US/SH/SL/FM/RM/PR designations, not subdivision-style street names. "Still Forest Dr" is a subdivision residential street in rural Cedar Creek, matching the naming pattern of county-maintained or private subdivision roads.
