## 3. Texas LGC Ch. 232 — Platting Statute

**§232.001(a) — Plat Required** (quoted via texas.public.law, retrieved 2026-04-08):
> "The owner of a tract of land located outside the limits of a municipality must have a plat of the subdivision prepared if the owner divides the tract into two or more parts to lay out: (1) a subdivision of the tract, including an addition; (2) lots; or (3) streets, alleys, squares, parks, or other parts of the tract intended by the owner of the tract to be dedicated to public use."

**§232.0015 — Exceptions** (relevant subsections, retrieved 2026-04-08):
- **(a):** "To determine whether specific divisions of land are required to be platted, a county may define and classify the divisions."
- **(c):** No plat required if land used primarily for agricultural/wildlife/timber use AND owner does not lay out §232.001(a)(3) features.
- **(f):** No plat required if "all of the lots of the subdivision are more than 10 acres in area" and no §232.001(a)(3) features.

## 4. Likely Path for This Parcel
