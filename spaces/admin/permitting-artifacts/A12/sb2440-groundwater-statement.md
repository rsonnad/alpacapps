## 🔑 Key finding (correction to task description)

SB 2440 is from the **Texas 88th Legislature, Regular Session (2023)** — NOT 87R (2021). It amended LGC §212.0101 (municipal plats) and **§232.0032 (county plats)**. Effective **January 1, 2024**, groundwater availability certification is **mandatory** (previously optional) for subdivision plats where groundwater is the primary water source. Bastrop County falls under §232.0032.

## 1. Statutory requirement

Per SB 2440, a county plat application for a subdivision using groundwater must include "a statement that: (1) is prepared by an engineer licensed to practice in this state or a geoscientist licensed to practice in this state; and (2) certifies that adequate groundwater is available for the subdivision."

TCEQ implementing rules: **30 TAC Chapter 230**. Form: **TCEQ-20962**.

## 2. Groundwater district

**Lost Pines Groundwater Conservation District (LPGCD)** — covers Bastrop and Lee Counties; created by SB 1911 (76th Leg., 1999), confirmed by voters Nov 2002. Primary aquifer: **Carrizo-Wilcox**. Office: 317 E. Hempstead St., Giddings, TX 78942.

## 3. ⚠️ WAIVER POSSIBILITY (important for this project)

Under **LGC §232.0032(a-1)**, the Commissioners Court **may waive** the certification requirement if there is credible evidence of sufficient groundwater AND *either*:
- (a) the tract is served by **Gulf Coast or Carrizo-Wilcox aquifer** (Cedar Creek sits over Carrizo-Wilcox — this likely applies), OR
- (b) the subdivision creates **no more than 10 lots**.

**At 1.7348 ac with a commercial IDP (likely ≤10 lots), this property probably qualifies for waiver on BOTH grounds.** Strong recommendation: **request waiver first** before commissioning a PE/PG certification.

## 4. Required contents of the statement (per statute + 30 TAC 230)

1. Prepared and signed/sealed by a **Texas-licensed PE or PG**
2. Certifies **adequate groundwater is available** for the subdivision
3. Based on site-specific evidence (well yield test, aquifer identification, projected annual demand vs. sustainable yield)
4. Submitted on TCEQ Form 20962 with the plat application
