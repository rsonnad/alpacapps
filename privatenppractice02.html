California PMHNP Requirements

The checklist for opening a psychiatric NP practice in the Bay Area.

🇫🇸 Licensure & Authority

1
RN License — Active California RN license via BRN
2
NP Certification — ANCC or AANP PMHNP board certification
3
Furnishing Number — Required to prescribe (CA BRN)
4
DEA Registration — For controlled substances
5
AB 890 — Path to full independent practice. By 2026, ~2/3 of CA NPs expected to qualify.

📋 Insurance Credentialing

1
NPI Number — Apply at NPPES (free, ~10 days)
2
CAQH Profile — Universal credentialing database
3
PECOS Enrollment — Required for Medicare
4
Malpractice Insurance — $1M/$3M coverage (~$800-1,500/yr)
5
Panel Applications — Apply per payer (60-120 days each)
