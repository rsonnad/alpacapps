## Goal

Get the property officially approved as a **commercial lodging development** by Bastrop County through the Infrastructure Development Plan (IDP) process, culminating in **Commissioners Court approval** and a **Certificate of Compliance**.

This is SEPARATE from (and prerequisite to) any building permit for the bathroom building.

- Shipping containers: 40x8

**Setback concern:** 10x42 trailer is ~1' off the back property line (county requires 15' for lodging units). Will need variance or relocation.

---

## CRITICAL: Construction Already Started

The bathroom building's first floor is already complete. Per Section III of Bastrop County's Lodging/RV Infrastructure Requirements: *"Construction may NOT begin before IDP is approved by Commissioners Court."* This is classified as a **Class B misdemeanor**.

**Strategy:**
1. Disclose proactively at the pre-development meeting — honesty builds credibility
2. Frame as motivation for fast-tracking compliance
3. Ask about retroactive permitting / voluntary compliance paths
4. The pre-dev meeting is free and non-binding — use it to gauge county's stance
